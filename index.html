Quantitative engine — required inputs

Traffic volumes and growth · toll tariff and indexation · CAPEX schedule · OPEX and maintenance · debt amount, pricing and tenor · tax and working-capital assumptions.

Once inputs are verified, a deterministic Python model can calculate project cash flows, debt sizing, DSCR, LLCR, equity IRR and sensitivities. The language model should explain outputs, not manufacture them.

Potential IFC questions

  • Is there a financing gap or need for longer tenor?
  • Could IFC participation mobilise additional private capital?
  • Are there material E&S, integrity or governance issues?
  • Is the potential role consistent with country and sector priorities?
  • What additionality would IFC bring?

What AI does

  • Extracts and summarises relevant information.
  • Classifies investors, sectors, geographies and projects.
  • Highlights inconsistencies, missing information and diligence questions.
  • Drafts investor briefs and meeting notes.

What AI must not do

  • Invent traffic, CAPEX, IRR, DSCR or debt terms.
  • Make credit, integrity, E&S or investment decisions.
  • Present assumptions as facts or infer investor appetite.
  • Replace due diligence or professional judgement.
