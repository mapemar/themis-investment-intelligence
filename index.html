Quantitative engine — required inputs

Traffic volumes and growth · toll tariff and indexation · CAPEX schedule · OPEX and maintenance · debt amount, pricing and tenor · tax and working-capital assumptions.

Once inputs are verified, a deterministic Python model can calculate project cash flows, debt sizing, DSCR, LLCR, equity IRR and sensitivities. The language model should explain outputs, not manufacture them.

Module 07 · Deterministic analytical engine

Project finance lab

A transparent toll-road / infrastructure debt-sizing sandbox. Enter your own assumptions to calculate debt service, annual DSCR and LLCR. No EcoNoroeste financial data are preloaded.

Illustrative assumptions only

Financing assumptions

The prefilled numbers are synthetic demonstration assumptions, not project facts or market benchmarks. All monetary values are in the same user-selected currency unit (millions).

Run the calculator to display the financing structure and metrics.

Methodology

  • Debt amount = project cost × debt share.
  • Annual debt service = level-payment annuity at the stated interest rate and tenor.
  • DSCR = annual CFADS / annual debt service.
  • LLCR = present value of CFADS over the debt tenor / opening debt.

Model limitations

  • Constant annual CFADS; no construction ramp-up or seasonality.
  • No taxes, working capital, reserve accounts, fees, grace period or balloon.
  • No sculpting or refinancing; annual debt service is level.
  • Not a credit model, valuation, bankability opinion or investment recommendation.

Design references: the project-finance functionality described in the Financial Analysis Toolkit and the toll-road BOT model. THEMIS implements its own transparent calculations; it does not copy their workbook, code or data.

What AI does

  • Extracts and summarises relevant information.
  • Classifies investors, sectors, geographies and projects.
  • Highlights inconsistencies, missing information and diligence questions.
  • Drafts investor briefs and meeting notes.

What AI must not do

  • Invent traffic, CAPEX, IRR, DSCR or debt terms.
  • Make credit, integrity, E&S or investment decisions.
  • Present assumptions as facts or infer investor appetite.
  • Replace due diligence or professional judgement.
